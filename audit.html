Before we begin

A few practical details.

What do you need from our team?+

Access to the analytics tools and dashboards in scope, relevant tracking plans and KPI definitions, and a point of contact across Product, Engineering, or Data. We agree the minimum access needed during scoping.

Will you change our production tracking?+

The audit is a diagnostic engagement. Implementation is scoped separately through Reliability Repair, with changes agreed before work begins.

Do we need to change analytics platforms?+

No. We work with your existing setup, including Amplitude, GA4, Mixpanel, PostHog, Looker, and similar tools. The goal is to make the evidence reliable, not to sell you a replacement stack.

What does the audit cost?+

The fee is agreed upfront based on the product journeys, tools, and access requirements in scope. The review conversation helps us define a focused engagement before you commit.

What happens after the audit?+

Your team can use the prioritized repair plan independently, or work with Proteus on Reliability Repair and ongoing analytics QA. There is no requirement to continue into another engagement.
